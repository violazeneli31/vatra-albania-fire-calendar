OCHA Common Operational Dataset - Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB), Albania, 2019 (alb_adm_2019b)
Source: Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX), https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-alb
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations (CC BY-IGO)

Files in this folder:
- ocha-ALB-ADM1-2019.geojson : 12 counties (qarqe)
- ocha-ALB-ADM2-2019.geojson : 61 municipalities (bashki, since the 2015 reform)

Why: geoBoundaries ADM2 for Albania holds the 37 older districts (rrethe), not the 61 municipalities.
VATRA uses geoBoundaries ADM0 for the country border and these OCHA files for counties and municipalities.
Some municipality names in the source are in genitive form or swapped (for example AL1101 is labelled
"Kavajë" but is Kamëz); config.py (MUNICIPALITY_NAMES) holds the corrected names by P-code.

Downloaded 14 Nov 2026 (shapefile converted to GeoJSON, attributes kept: names EN/SQ and P-codes).
